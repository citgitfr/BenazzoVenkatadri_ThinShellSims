Claude (for Federico)
- design_screen.py: buckling estimates for TRAC / OM / CO sections in compression (before: tape springs
  only, so no TRAC/OM/CO design was ever rejected for buckling).
    P_col = Euler with the weaker EI (K = 1 pinned, 0.5 clamped).
    P_loc = KNOCKDOWN x classical curved-panel stress of the flattest arc (largest R) x total area;
            KNOCKDOWN 0.3 for OM/CO (closed walls, both edges supported, as for TS) and 0.027 for TRAC
            (free-edge flanges), calibrated on it001 (L038/Y005/R014 buckled LOCALLY at 49 N: the mode is
            0.84 local distortion vs 0.075 rigid-section motion; Euler load 19 kN; 0.3-classical 542 N).
  Screen re-run for the study with --max-rows 100000 (the 20000-row cap had cut off every 0.14-0.175 mm
  design): 57318 rows pass.
- study.py: buckling calibration per section type (FE/estimate median per TS/TRAC/OM/CO, falling back to
  all runs), because the estimates are different formulas; "B STALL" results now enter the calibration.
- shell_buckling.py: adaptive path steps (_next_step): after a step that converged easily the next one
  doubles, up to max_step (only when max_step is given; otherwise unchanged). Step A: "easily" = <= 3
  iterations. Step B (imperfect_path, ASDShellQ4 needs 8+ iterations from the start): <= first-step
  iterations + 2; beyond fine_from (0.7 x the A critical parameter) B goes back to the base step so the
  peak is resolved as before; max_param ends the path at the range end.
  Validation (perf/ex): tape-spring compression A 45.6229 vs 45.6235 N, 28 s vs 87 s; opposite bending A
  identical (311.481 N mm); B opposite bending identical (401.99 N mm at 0.145).
- shell_buckling.parallel_stability_scan: option `enough`: when the stable end of the coarse bracket already
  carries this load, the refinement rounds (each re-marches the whole path) are skipped and A is reported
  as a lower bound with its bracket ("A ... STABLE to X (lower bound; crossing in bracket [X, Y])").
- study.py: max_step = 8 x step for A and B (STEP_GROWTH); A max_iter 30 -> 10 (ShellNLDKGQ converges in
  2-3 iterations when it converges at all; a failing attempt cost 30 x 110 s on it003 before the step cut);
  A enough = 1.5 x the required buckling load (A_ENOUGH) for threshold requirements.
- Why the buckling runs were slow (measured on it003, OM M008, 2.2 mm mesh, 317,388 DOFs, 1 CPU):
  model build 7 s; one path step 213-233 s (2 Newton iterations, ~110 s each: tangent + UmfPack sparse
  factorization); one eigenvalue solve of K_T 1376 s (23 min; OpenSees eigen = ARPACK on a BAND matrix,
  ~10 GB, the only eigen solvers in this build are band/dense: genBandArpack, symmBandLapack,
  symmGenLapack, fullGenLapack); 8 BLAS threads gave no speedup (same 213 s and 1375 s), so more CPUs
  per process do not help - only more processes. The old A scan's farthest point marched 60 steps and
  then stalled near 1446 N, where each failed attempt ran 30 iterations (55 min) before the step was
  halved: > 12 h for one scan point.
- Verification chain (done by a subagent, approved by Federico 11/9/26): compare_results.py read Abaqus
  SK1/SK2 as kxx/kyy, but Abaqus 2026 names SK by the axis bent ABOUT (SK1 = kyy, SK2 = kxx); it rebuilt
  N/M with the single laminate on the doubled webs; and Abaqus wrote stresses only at 3 section points,
  so the 90-deg ply could not be checked. Fixed: case_to_abaqus.py writes S/E at every section point of
  every ply (flange and web sets), compare_results.py pairs section points with plies and uses the doubled
  web laminate. Tests: test_verification.py 29/29, regression 359/359 (10 new T20 checks); real Abaqus
  rerun of it001: kxx -0.79%, kyy -1.89%, web N11 -0.64%, 90-deg ply s2 within 0.4%.
  NOT INSTALLED: the protocol hook blocks writes to .claude/verification/ during a study; the fixed
  compare_results.py, odb_mock.py and test_verification.py are staged in the study's perf/verif_fix/
  verification/ and must be copied over .claude/verification/ (by Federico, or after the study closes).
- study.py BUCKLING_CUTOFF 3.0 -> 1.5 (Federico's decision): for a threshold buckling requirement B stops at
  1.5 x the required load. shell_buckling.imperfect_path option max_load: the path stops once the load
  factor reaches it; boom_buckling passes the case's B_cutoff (before, the cutoff only shortened the path
  RANGE, to 2 x the linear shortening at the cutoff load, so B ran on to ~2 x the cutoff). Tested:
  tape-spring compression with B_cutoff 30 N stops at 32.2 N (5 steps, 20 s).
  study.py resume_A also recognises the new "A ... STABLE to X at Y (lower bound ...); T s" line.
- Regression suite after all of the above: 359 checks, 359 passed, 0 failed (868 s; log

- shell-bc-load (shell_bc_load.py, linear_solve and the LINEAR_CODE piece of assembled scripts): when the

- Fast buckling pipeline (Federico's decision, 11/13/26). Evidence: Code for Project/11-12-26 (Federico)/
  coarse_mode_study/SUMMARY.md.
  Findings:
    - The B strength is insensitive to the imperfection shape at amplitude 0.1 t: within 0.2% over 10 runs on 3
      cases, even with a mode of MAC 0, on omega it005 and both tape cases.
    - The old step A cost 3.4-6.7 h per design.
    - On omega it005, a 4 mm B mesh follows the 2.2 mm path to 0.01% up to the requirement, but stalls later
      (591 N vs 528 N; 3 mm: 549 N).
  New shell-buckling/segment_mode.py (new file):
    - Imperfection seed from a dense classical LBA (Kg by finite difference of the ShellNLDKGQ tangent) on at
      most 8000 DOFs.
    - The model is the whole member if it fits at 4 x 4-8 mm; otherwise a clamped segment at 4 x 8 mm, whose
      mode is repeated along the length and mapped onto the B mesh.
    - In compression, if the Euler load is below the segment's local load, a coarse nonlinear stability scan
      of the whole member also runs and its global mode is added.
    - Cost: 45-90 s.
  boom_buckling.py: optional case key "mode_label" (label of the imperfection in the result; default "mode A",
  unchanged).
  study.py, run_buckling -> run_buckling_fast when BUCKLING_PIPELINE = 'fast' (new default) and the case has a
  B block:
    - No step A (its range block stays in buckling.json, unused).
    - The seed is cached per section / L / load / B mesh in <study>/buckling_seeds/.
    - Threshold requirement with a design mesh finer than B_MESH_SIZE = 4 mm: B runs on 4 mm and must reach
      B_COARSE_MARGIN = 1.12 x the requirement.
    - If the 4 mm path ends between 1x and 1.12x, B is re-run automatically on the design mesh with the cutoff
      at the requirement, and that result counts.
    - If the 4 mm path ends below the requirement, the design fails (the coarse mesh only overestimates).
    - Design mesh >= 4 mm: B on the design mesh, cutoff at the requirement.
    - "maximize buckling": design mesh, no cutoff.
    - The custom option buckling_objective "A" is unchanged. BUCKLING_PIPELINE = 'legacy' restores the old
      pipeline.
    - New design.json fields: buckling_pipeline, buckling_B_size, buckling_seed, buckling_fine_check,
      buckling_time_s. finalize also copies buckling_run.json and its picture.
  Tests:
