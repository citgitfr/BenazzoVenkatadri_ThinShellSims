We wanted to add torsion as one of our load cases that we can use in the optimization.  Our existing shell-bc-load skill allows for linear torsion studies of small twist angles, but it breaks down when we have larger twist angles that go into the nonlinear regime.  I tested a torsion problem for an omega boom (which stays in the linear regime) and a TRAC boom (which is open cross-section and has much larger twists that go into the nonlinear regime) to make sure that the linear and nonlinear solvers work.  I also ran the Abaqus verification agent to ensure that the OpenSeesPy results were consistent with Abaqus (i.e. that the torsion simulation is being done correctly in OpenSeesPy).  Once I was satisfied with the nonlinear torsion capability of the system, I had Claude add it as part of the shell-bc-load skill.
