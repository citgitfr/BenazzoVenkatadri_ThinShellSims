[2] What OpenSeesPy 3.8 exposes for a stability (eigen) analysis - probes on a
    500 x 10 x 1 mm Al strip column (E = 70 GPa), pinned via rigid ends,
    Euler P_cr = pi^2 E I / L^2 = 2.3029 N
- printA('-ret') returns the assembled tangent ONLY with system FullGeneral
  (dense; every other system returns an empty list). nodeDOFs gives equation
  numbers. eleResponse(e, 'stiffness') is not implemented for shells.
- eigen('-standard', '-symmBandLapack', n) works after a static step and gives
  the n smallest eigenvalues of the CURRENT tangent K_T (checked against the
  dense printA matrix: identical to 4 digits). Default ARPACK only solves the
  generalized problem (needs mass); -fullGenLapack returned inf.
- Pitfall: with NormDispIncr 1e-12 the step did not converge, the domain
  reverted to zero load and the "loaded" K_T was K0 (first, wrong conclusion
  that Kg = 0). Tolerance 1e-8 converges in 2 iterations.
- KEY FINDING: smallest eigenvalue of K_T vs load P/P_cr = 0, 0.5, 0.9:
    ASDShellQ4 -corotational : 3.031e-4, 3.033e-4, 3.034e-4  (no change!)
    ShellNLDKGQ              : 3.030e-4, 1.515e-4, 3.03e-5   (-> 0 at P_cr, linear)
    ShellDKGQ, ShellMITC4    : constant (linear elements, expected)
  ASDShellQ4 corotational has a correct (geometrically nonlinear) residual -
  with a lateral perturbation the midspan deflection grows as 1/(1 - P/P_cr) -
  but its tangent has NO initial-stress (geometric) stiffness: Newton
  iterations grow 9 -> 43 as P -> 0.7 P_cr (modified-Newton convergence) and
  the eigenvalues of K_T say nothing about stability. ShellNLDKGQ has a
  consistent tangent: its lowest eigenvalue falls linearly to zero exactly
  at the Euler load.
- Consequence for the method:
  (A) stability (eigen) analysis = ShellNLDKGQ: follow the equilibrium path,
      compute the lowest eigenvalues of K_T at each converged state, and find
      the load where the smallest one crosses zero (the OpenSees counterpart of
      the Abaqus "preload + eigenvalue" approach of the MDPI paper).
  (B) path following with an imperfection (Riks-type): any element, including
      ASDShellQ4 laminates with off-axis plies; the peak of the load path is
      the buckling strength; the imperfection shape comes from (A).

[3] Literature survey (sub-agent, web; details and sources in BUCKLING_STUDY.md)
- MDPI Biomimetics 9:494 (Palmeri & Laurenzi 2024) is about a CTM boom
  (two omega shells, 500 mm, [+-45 GFPW/0 CF/+-45 GFPW]); read through the
  authors' open-access copy (MDPI blocked here). Abaqus S4R, MPC end
  reference points; "nonlinear buckling load" = (1) linear eigen buckling,
  (2) load-controlled nonlinear static run to that load (no stabilisation),
  (3) eigen buckling about a deformed state backed off until "close to but
  not in" post-buckling. Perfect geometry. Only Pareto maxima reported
  (Mx 11.71 N m, My 10.27 N m, F 545 N): weak benchmark to reproduce.
- Leclerc & Pellegrino 2020 (IJSS 203) use the same 3 steps + Riks with a
  first-mode imperfection of 0.2 t; Bessa & Pellegrino 2018: Riks with
  mode-combination imperfections. Royer, Hutchinson & Pellegrino 2022:
  bifurcations along the nonlinear fundamental path (isotropic longerons).
- Closed form for tape springs: steady fold moments M+* = (1 + nu) D alpha,
  M-* = -(1 - nu) D alpha (Calladine; Seffen & Pellegrino 1999 Eqs 2.29-31);
  orthotropic: M+- = +-(sqrt(D11 D22) +- D12) alpha (Yee et al. 2004).
- Seffen & Pellegrino tape: CuBe E = 131 GPa, nu = 0.3, R = 13.3 mm,
  alpha = 1.85 rad, t = 0.1 mm, L = 200 mm. Peak moments only in plots;
  M+max depends on L, |M-max| does not.
- opstool's LBA uses K_T(after a reference step) - K0 with ShellNLDKGT; a
  forum thread confirms eigen with M = 1 does not give buckling loads.

[6] Eigen solver speed

  DOFs and > 20 min at 11.8k DOFs (tape column run killed). Default ARPACK
  ('-genBandArpack', shift-invert about 0) with a unit mass on every DOF

  3.2k and 1.9 s at 11.8k DOFs. tangent_eigen() now uses it. ('PythonSparse'
  eigen does not exist in 3.8.)

   (14.4818) mm from the arc centre; J = 0.0082 mm^4.
   L = 300 (flexural-torsional 16.407 N): A 17.617 (2 mm), 17.606 (1.25 mm): +7.3%

  The L = 1200 mode is pure z-translation of the section (mean uz = 1),
  i.e. the right mode, but 31% too high and mesh-converged.
  1 / mean(cos^2 phi) over the arc facets (|phi| <= 0.925 rad) = 1.315:
  ShellNLDKGQ's geometric stiffness only contains the gradients of the
  displacement NORMAL to each facet (plate / von Karman type), not the
  in-plane ones. Exact for flat plates and strips (part 1), wrong for global
  modes of curved sections where the section translates partly in the plane
  of its facets. Confirmed with a fresh model and ONE eigen call per load
  level (mu_min linear in P, zero at 1.295 P_th for L = 1200, 1.07 for 300):
  it is the element, not the path.

[8] Rigid ends for rotations: rigidLink + Transformation is broken here
- Tip moment on the tape spring (rigid ends via shell_bc_load.rigid_end):
  linear solve fine (rotation 0.25% from M L / E I), but rotation control
  (DisplacementControl on ry of the master) diverges with ShellNLDKGQ and
  gives ZERO moment with ASDShellQ4; load control gives a 50% too large
  rotation at 4 N mm. With constraints('Penalty') all three are right.
  rigidLink is also a linear (small-rotation) constraint. New
  sb.rigid_end(mesh, edge, E, t): stiff elasticBeamColumn "spokes" (E_b =
  1000 E, Corotational transformation) from the master to each edge node:
  rotation control M/theta = 3967 (NLDKGQ) / 4063 (ASD) N mm/rad vs
  beam theory 4102 (3 mm mesh), load and rotation control consistent.
  (The strip columns of [7] were axial only: rigidLink was fine there.)

[9] ShellNLDKGQ state is changed by an eigen call
- Rotation control of the tape, 0.004 rad steps: without eigen calls
  15.38, 30.74, 46.04, 61.26 N mm; with an eigen call after each step every
  later step fails (wipeAnalysis + new analysis, band solver: same). A
  LoadControl(0) Newton step after the eigen call re-converges, but to a
  different equilibrium (rotation 0.00400 -> 0.00388 at the same moment):
  the element state was modified. ASDShellQ4 is not affected.
- New sb.stability_scan(build, step, max_param): every evaluation rebuilds
  the model, marches to the path parameter without eigen calls, then ONE
  eigen call; coarse scan + Illinois regula falsi on mu_min(p).

[10] Method B with ASDShellQ4 needs load control for bifurcations
- DisplacementControl of the lateral deflection of the imperfect tape column
  diverged at step 1 (ASDShellQ4's tangent has no load-deflection coupling,
